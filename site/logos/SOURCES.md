# Logos

Logos shown next to formats, protocols, standards, data sources and ecosystems on the site.
Each logo is a trademark or mark of its owner, used only to identify the thing it names. They are
not covered by the Plug Map code (MIT) or data (CC BY 4.0) licences. Terms without a logo here get
a drawn tile (see `mark()` in `app.js`). Files are cropped to the logo with a small margin, and PNGs
are at most 256 px. To add or replace one, put `<term id>.svg` (or `.png`) in this folder, add it to
`LOGOS` in `app.js` (and to `WIDE` if it is wider than about 2:1), and add a row below.
